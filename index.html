Light and Dark

Every colour is a token with a light and a dark value, so the whole page follows the visitor's system setting. The button at the top of the page switches it by hand and remembers the choice. To force a theme, set data-theme="dark" or data-theme="light" on the <html> element.

Spacing

u-margin-1 to u-margin-6 add top and bottom margin in steps of 0.7rem, for example on a container that holds a section. Neighbouring margins collapse to the larger one.
